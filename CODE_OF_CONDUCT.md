# Code of conduct

Doors YU Club projects are places to learn and collaborate. Be respectful, explain feedback constructively, and make room for members with different levels of experience. Harassment, personal attacks, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

If conduct in a project makes you uncomfortable, contact club leadership through the club's official channel. The club leadership will decide how to handle reports under applicable university rules. Do not post personal details in a public issue.
