DOORS is a student-led professional development club at Al Yamamah University, built to help students explore opportunities, develop practical skills, and prepare for what comes after the classroom.

We bring students closer to professional experiences through workshops, initiatives, events, and opportunities to learn by building.

## What We Do

DOORS focuses on creating practical experiences that support students throughout their professional journey — from developing new skills and exploring career paths to connecting with opportunities and industry.

Our work includes:

- Professional development workshops and sessions
- Technical and career-focused initiatives
- Events and collaborative experiences
- Student-led projects
- Opportunities to learn, contribute, and build

## Tech & Digital at DOORS

This GitHub organization is home to the technology built by DOORS.

Here, our Tech & Digital Committee collaborates on the club's website, digital experiences, tools, automations, and technical initiatives.

We believe contributing should also be a learning experience. Members are encouraged to explore new technologies, take ownership of projects, and turn ideas into things students can actually use.
